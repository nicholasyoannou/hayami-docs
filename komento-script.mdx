KomentoScript is a more advanced format built on-top of what powers custom websites that tells Hayami what website, what pages on those sites to support, how to extract anime/episode info, and where to mount Hayami itself. KomentoScript allows you to sync from community instances (sources), allowing you to choose sites _you personally_ want, pre-configured by the community out-the-box. KomentoScript instances can be hosted by anyone.

## How to add KomentoScript instances

<Note>
  KomentoScript instances are community-managed and not affliated with Hayami directly.
</Note>

KomentoScript sources sync automatically on a weekly basis. If you want to add your own sources, you can do so at your own accord by going to **Settings \> KomentoScript \> "New source"** and adding the URL of your KomentoScript location.

To see all the sites the KomentoScript source has to offer, add `/all` to the end to see all those sites on the Hayami extension (see the community instance URL below as an example). Hayami also accepts manually importing KomentoScript files instead of via a URL to allow community sharing similar to custom websites.

Under **Settings \> KomentoScript**, you can manually sync by clicking **'Sync now'** if there was a recent update you want fetched.
