Feedback is heavily appreciated, and allows me to know what underlying problems there are with the extension itself, and helps illustrate what you want improved, or added.

Things to add as feedback could be: **things you dislike, things you want to keep seeing, and features or additions that you think'd be neat.** This allows me to continuously keep making the extension better & allows me to see what should be a focus if things are a tad wacko in some areas.

You can leave feedback in three ways.

- Via the extension popup: clicking the feedback icon, which'd embed a feedback form. If you don't wish to be contacted regarding feedback, please indicate so when popping the feedback form by. You can also submit the feedback anonymously if you wish.
- Via the Discord server, hyperlinked in the footer of the Hayami popup.
- Via email at [hi@hayami.moe](mailto:hi@hayami.moe).

If you appreciate the extension and want to support it monetarily, [you can do so also](https://hayami.moe/donate). This helps fund server costs, and maintenance of the extension itself.
