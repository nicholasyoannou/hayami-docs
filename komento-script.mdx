<Note>
  The current version of KomentoScript is **v0.1**.
</Note>

<Warning>
  KomentoScript is in early-phase undergoing testing & can be subject to change. Documentation will be more limited during initial development & testing periods.
</Warning>

KomentoScript is a JSON format that tells Hayami which pages to support, how to extract anime/episode info, and where to mount Hayami itself. For more details on creating your own KomentoScripts, please see [**KomentoScript (for script-makers)**](/komento-script-dev).

KomentoScript is provided by Hayami for official mappings to some sites. KomentoScript sources sync automatically on a weekly basis. Under Settings \> KomentoScript Sync, you can manually sync by clicking 'Sync now'. You can also add your own sources that adhere to the KomentoScript specification.

If you want to add your own sources, you can do so at your own accord by going to Settings \> KomentoScript Sync \> "New source" and adding the URL of your KomentoScript location. Hayami also accepts manually importing KomentoScript files instead of via a URL to allow community sharing similar to custom websites.
