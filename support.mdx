Hayami has a backend & third-party services that enables the extension to continually keep running. \
Primarily, this is composed of it's server-side mapper & databases that handles the majority of the background mapping work, ensuring your episode matches the corresponding comments post on the anime your watching. There's also object storage for archival content of images for specific discussion platforms.

As much as I'd like to maintain the project and continue to support it, I'd heavily appreciate monetary contributions to allow for continued maintenance to pay for server costs, and allow for continued features in the future.

You can do so at https://hayami.moe/donate. \
<Icon icon="handshake" />  **One-time**, or **monthly**, contributions are much appreciated.

You can cancel at any time, and there are no additional perks gained from donating, other than helping to support the projects continuation & development.
