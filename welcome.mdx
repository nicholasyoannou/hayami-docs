Hayami Komento brings communities to you, through comments straight underneath anime episodes. Hayami gives you choice on what **discussion platform**, **forum**, and **community** you look at by default. As per the [FAQ on-site](https://hayami.moe/faq), Hayami is a comments aggregator browser extension that shows comments from various platforms in one place. And all for free (though any monetary [support](/support) is much appreciated).

The purpose of this documentation is to provide detailed guides explaining _how_ you use and setup Hayami's features, as well as provide more in-depth documentation for tinkerers for various features. As per this writing, Hayami is in early beta (which means some features may contain bugs, may break at times, or may be unreliable), so [**feedback**](/feedback) is very much appreciated at any point you use the extension. You can provide feedback **through the extension popup** (by clicking the feedback icon at the very top), which allows you to leave feedback anonymously, and **also by** [**email**](mailto:hi@hayami.moe).

Currently, Hayami has support for a variety of discussion platforms, which currently are:

- Reddit (r/anime, with partial support for other subreddits)
- Disqus (Chuunime - [https://discussanime.moe](https://discussanime.moe))
- MyAnimeList (Forums)
- AniList (Forums)
- YouTube (i.e. Muse Asia, Ani-One Asia)
- <Tooltip tip="Archived (2016-2024) comments">Aniwave</Tooltip>
- The Anime Community

And possibly more to come in-future, as it's a continuous growing list and will expand as the extension continually develops over time. Alongside this, Hayami provides some features that aims to enhance your experience using the extension.
